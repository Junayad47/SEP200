1.Do you find lambda functions useful? Why or why not?
  
  Ans: Lambda can be 6.6x faster to compile than std::bind!. But, Data science particularly benefits from the use of lambda functions. 2.Where are lambda functions best utilized?

  Ans: In order to write simple code without wasting numerous lines defining a function, lambda functions are designed as a shorthand for doing so.   Since they lack a name unless given one, they are also referred to as anonymous functions.


3.What are the three components of the Standard Template Library?

  Ans: Containers (often used templatized data structures), iterators, and algorithms make up STL's three main building blocks.
